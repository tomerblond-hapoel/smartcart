FAQ

Common questions

Everything you need to know about SmartCart.

SmartCart is a group-buying platform where shoppers team up to unlock exclusive discounts. The more people join, the better the deal.

Browse products, join or create a group purchase. Once enough people join before the deadline, everyone can pay the discounted price.

You only pay after a group successfully reaches its participant target. There are no upfront charges — zero risk to you.

If the group doesn't reach minimum participants before the deadline, it's marked as failed and no one is charged.

Businesses get exposure, stronger conversion, and larger orders by allowing shoppers to buy together.
